Privacy Policy

Gantt Chart: Project Timeline (the "App") · Effective September 25, 2026 · 日本語

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information. No account is required, and no data is sent to the developer's servers. The App contains no advertising or analytics.

2. Data stored on your device

Projects, tasks, checklists, notes and reminder settings you enter are stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data.

3. Notifications

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. Notification content is never sent off the device. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Sharing with Google Drive

Only when you use the sharing feature, the contents of the project you choose (task names, dates, progress, assignees, notes and checklists) are saved as a file in a Google Drive location you select. The App reads and writes this file through the iOS Files feature and the Google Drive app; the developer has no access to its contents. You control who can access the file through Google Drive's sharing settings. For how Google handles data in Google Drive, please see Google's Privacy Policy. Reminder settings are not shared.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact cyoro01@gmail.com.
