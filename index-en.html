Gantt Chart: Project Timeline

Support page for the Gantt chart app for iPhone and iPad. 日本語

Contact
For bug reports and feature requests, email cyoro01@gmail.com. Please include your device (iPhone / iPad) and iOS version.

Features

  • Gantt chart for each project (day / week / month views)
  • Touch and hold a bar to move it; drag its ends to change dates
  • Completion checkmarks, progress, milestones, assignees and notes
  • Checklists inside each task (progress can follow the checklist) – Premium
  • Reminder notifications tied to start and end dates – Premium
  • Share and sync projects via Google Drive – Premium
  • Delay tracking and working-day durations that skip weekends and holidays – Premium

FAQ

Q. I'm not getting notifications.
Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Gantt Chart. Completed tasks (100% progress) and reminders in the past are not notified.

Q. Are any features paid?
The free version includes 1 project with up to 5 tasks. Premium, a one-time in-app purchase, unlocks unlimited projects and tasks, plus checklists, reminders and sharing. Everyone who shares needs Premium.

Q. What happens to checklists and reminders I used for free before?
If you have used the app since version 1.1 or earlier, checklists and reminders stay free for you. If you have used it since version 1.2 or earlier, you keep unlimited projects and tasks. If you already bought Sharing, you have Premium.

Q. Premium stopped working after I changed devices or reinstalled.
Open the purchase screen from the share button and tap "Restore Purchase". You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Q. How do I share a project with someone?
Tap the share button at the top right of the project screen, choose "Save to Google Drive and start sharing", and pick Google Drive as the location. Then, in the Google Drive app, share that file with the other person's email address as an "Editor". They add the file to "My Drive" and open it with the app's import button (↓).

Q. How do I count durations without weekends or holidays?
Tap the calendar button at the top of the project screen and turn on "Weekends off" and/or "Japanese public holidays off" (Premium). Days off are shaded and durations count working days.

Q. Some tasks have a red outline.
They are 10 or more points behind where they should be today (Premium). Tasks past their end date that aren't done are marked "Overdue". Updating their progress clears it.

Q. "Google Drive" doesn't appear as a save location.
Install the Google Drive app and sign in, then turn on Google Drive in the Files app under "Browse" → "…" → "Edit".

Q. Shared changes don't show up right away.
How quickly changes reach the other device depends on the Google Drive app's syncing (usually a few seconds to a few tens of seconds). Opening the Google Drive app once can speed it up.

Q. Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
